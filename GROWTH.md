# TechGnomo Organic Growth System

## Goal

Attract qualified small-business owners and first-time founders who need one of three outcomes:

1. a clearer, faster website or landing page;
2. a focused prototype or MVP;
3. a practical AI-assisted workflow.

The objective is qualified enquiries and product interest, not raw visits.

## Current acquisition assets

- The homepage explains the three services and directs visitors to one project brief.
- ClearMoneyPath demonstrates mobile product execution through a real working MVP.
- AppStart demonstrates product strategy, prompt architecture and AI evaluation.
- The free MVP Scope Checker creates an evergreen reason to visit, share and link to TechGnomo.

## Zero-budget publishing loop

Use one real build decision each week as the source. Publish it once on the website, then adapt it into:

- one LinkedIn post;
- one short vertical-video script;
- one GitHub README or release note update;
- one useful answer in a relevant community where the question already exists.

Every adaptation should point to the most relevant product or tool page, not automatically to the homepage.

## Search themes to validate with real data

Do not mass-produce pages for these phrases. Use Search Console impressions and genuine customer questions to decide what deserves a useful page.

- free MVP scope checker
- how to reduce MVP features
- app prototype for first-time founders Australia
- landing page design for small business Australia
- AI automation for small business workflows
- pay-cycle budget planner Australia
- how to calculate safe-to-spend money

## Weekly measurement

Track:

- search impressions and clicks by page and query;
- click-through rate for queries already ranking;
- visits to product pages and the scope checker;
- completed scope checks;
- copied or emailed briefs;
- direct project enquiries.

Ignore vanity metrics that do not move a visitor toward a useful action.

## TechGnomo Growth Agent — phase two

Build the agent only after Search Console and analytics contain enough real data. Its safe responsibilities:

- collect query and landing-page performance;
- find high-impression, low-click opportunities;
- identify missing internal links and stale product status;
- draft evidence-led content briefs from real projects;
- repurpose approved source content for social channels;
- produce a weekly priority report;
- prepare changes for human review before publication.

The agent must never generate fake visits, clicks, reviews, comments, followers or backlinks. It must not mass-post generic AI content or publish unsupported claims.
